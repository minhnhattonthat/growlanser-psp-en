- references/platform-psp.md (PSMF movies): when re-encoding GOPs of a .pmf, keep every access unit at its original offset and byte size (new slice + filler NAL type 12 / zero padding). Moving AU boundaries inside a GOP, even with every PTS-packet start kept and a stream ffmpeg decodes with 0 errors, made PPSSPP's sceMpeg playback smear from the first edited GOP and end the movie early. Near-static AUs (~10-byte all-skip slices) can't be matched by x264 (a byte longer): keep the original AU and feed x264 the previous frame again. libx264 through ffmpeg ignores `qpfile` (CLI-only) silently; `zones=a,b,q=N` gave odd sizes; use one QP per GOP. Port: tools/pmflogo.py.
